1. Overview

Swerve is a single-player puzzle game. It has no accounts and no chat or user-generated content. It shows ads and offers optional in-app purchases. This policy explains what data the game and the services it uses collect, and why.

2. What stays on your device

Your stars, best scores, streak, hints, purchases already credited and settings are saved only on your device (in the app's local storage). Uninstalling the app or using Profile → Reset progress deletes them. We do not run any server of our own and receive none of this data.

3. Anonymous gameplay statistics (optional)

The first time you play, Swerve asks whether you want to share anonymous gameplay statistics. Nothing is sent unless you tap Share stats, and you can turn it off at any time in Profile → Share anonymous stats. If you agree, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to record:

  • which levels you start, finish, restart or leave, and how long they take;
  • stars earned, ink used and hints used;
  • chapters unlocked and completed, and daily challenges played;
  • settings changes (sound, haptics) and store or ad interactions;
  • basic device and app information collected by Firebase (device model, operating system version, app version, country and language, and a random app-instance identifier).

These statistics are used only to understand which levels are too easy or too hard and to improve the game. Google keeps this event data for up to 14 months and then deletes it automatically.

4. Advertising

Swerve shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the menus, occasionally a full-screen ad between levels, and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch (for an extra hint, a level skip, another daily challenge try or a streak save). Players in Iran, where AdMob serves no ads, see ads from Adivery instead.

To show and measure ads, these networks may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), device and app information, and ad interaction data. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU, UK and Switzerland), Swerve asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown; you can change your choice at any time in Profile → Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

5. In-app purchases

Swerve offers two optional purchases: Remove Ads (one-time) and a Hints Pack. Payments are handled entirely by the store you installed the game from (Google Play, Cafe Bazaar or Myket). We never see your payment details; the game only receives a purchase confirmation so it can credit what you bought.

6. Sharing and sale of data

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with the service providers above (Google Firebase, Google AdMob, Adivery and your app store) for the purposes described. All data is sent encrypted (HTTPS). Swerve does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

7. Permissions

  • Vibration: haptic feedback during play.
  • Internet / network state: loading ads, purchases and the optional statistics.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ad networks to show and measure ads.
  • Billing: in-app purchases through your app store.

8. Your choices and data deletion

  • Turn statistics off in Profile → Share anonymous stats.
  • Change ad consent in Profile → Ad privacy choices, or reset your advertising ID in device settings.
  • Delete all game data on the device with Profile → Reset progress or by uninstalling the app.
  • To ask for deletion of statistics linked to your app-instance identifier, email us at the address below.

9. Children

Swerve contains no mature content, but it is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. Ads are not personalized for users identified as under the age of consent.
